About

A contemporary platform for art, design and community.

Sienna Arts brings together artistic practice, design, creative learning and collaboration, creating space for ideas, people and creative experiences to meet.

Why Sienna Arts exists.

Sienna Arts grew from a simple idea: creative practice becomes more meaningful when it can be experienced, discussed and shared.

We create space for artists and designers to develop work, for people to encounter creative ideas, and for communities to participate through projects, learning and collaboration.

Art

Art at Sienna Arts includes artist projects, exhibitions, plein-air practice, creative learning and shared artistic experiences.
